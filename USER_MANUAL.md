# Soniclet - Official User Manual

Welcome to **Soniclet**! This manual will help you understand all the powerful features and smart gestures built into the app, designed to give you a magical music experience.

---

## 🚀 Core Features (New Updates!)

### 1. Instant Loading (Zero Wait Time)
Soniclet uses a cutting-edge local **Room Database** to remember your entire music library. 
* **How it works:** Instead of scanning your storage every time you open the app, it instantly fetches data from its smart cache. You will never see a "Loading..." spinner again!

### 2. Smart Background Sync
* **How it works:** Soniclet has an automated background worker that quietly checks your phone for new songs or deleted tracks every 12 hours. 
* **Benefit:** Your library is always up-to-date automatically, and because it uses Android's official WorkManager, it consumes almost 0% extra battery.

### 3. Memory & Battery Optimized
* Whether you have an older phone or a flagship, Soniclet lazy-loads album art and compresses data so that it uses minimum RAM and battery.

## 🎛️ Smart Gestures & Controls

Soniclet is built for one-handed and intuitive use. Try these gestures:

### Global App Navigation
* **1-Finger Edge Swipe:** Swipe inward from the left edge of your screen to instantly go back or close settings.
* **2-Finger Swipe:** Swipe with two fingers anywhere to jump back to the Home/Library screen.
* **2-Finger Tap:** Tap anywhere with two fingers to Play or Pause the music instantly.
* **3-Finger Swipe (L/R):** Swipe left or right with three fingers to skip to the Next or Previous track.

### Full-Screen Player
* **Right Edge Volume Slide:** On the player screen, slide your finger up or down along the right edge to change the volume smoothly.
* **Long Press & Drag:** Long press on the album cover and drag left or right to seek forward or backward in the song.

### Mini-Player (Bottom Bar)
* **Swipe Up:** Skip to the Next Track.
* **Swipe Down:** Go back to the Previous Track.
* **Drag Left / Right:** Seek the song's position directly on the mini-player without opening the full player.

## 🪄 Motion & Smart Environment

Enable these in the Settings to make your phone feel alive:

* **Pocket Mode:** The app uses your phone's proximity sensor. Put the phone in your pocket to pause the music, and take it out to resume.
* **Air Wave (Jedi Swipe):** Wave your hand over the top of the phone screen to Play/Pause or Skip tracks hands-free!
* **Pick Up to Play:** Lift your phone from a table to automatically wake up the music player.
* **Shake Phone:** Give your device a quick shake to shuffle or skip tracks.
* **Flip Face-Down:** Place your phone screen-down on a table to instantly pause playback.
* **Tilt to Seek:** Tilt your phone to the right to fast-forward the song like magic.
